WhareScore Badge · Chrome extension

You’re ready to read every NZ listing through a risk lens.

The badge annotates listings on homes.co.nz, OneRoof, and realestate.co.nz with the same risk analysis that powers wharescore.co.nz. Nothing from the listing page is copied or stored — only the address is sent to WhareScore.

1Getting started

  1. Step 01

    Pin the badge

    Open Chrome’s puzzle-piece menu and pin WhareScore Badge. The popup gives you per-site toggles and a 24-hour pause.

  2. Step 02

    Visit a supported listing

    Open any address page on homes.co.nz, OneRoof, or realestate.co.nz. A floating badge slides in at the bottom-right with that property’s WhareScore.

  3. Step 03

    Dig deeper

    Click View full report for the complete risk analysis. Sign in to save the property; upgrade to Pro for price + rent estimates, walk score, and PDF export.

2Supported sites

  • homes.co.nz — listing pages under /address/*
  • OneRoof — listing pages under /property/*
  • realestate.co.nz — listing pages under /residential/sale/*
  • Trade Me — temporarily disabled while we verify the badge mounts correctly against the Angular-rendered page.

3Your controls

  • Click the toolbar icon to pause the badge for 24 hours.
  • Toggle any site off in the popup — the badge stops appearing there.
  • Click × on the badge to dismiss it for that specific address (remembered for 7 days).
  • Drag the badge by its header to reposition it — we remember where you put it per site.
  • Uninstall via Chrome’s standard extension manager. All extension state is discarded on uninstall.

4What we do not collect

We do not collect listing content (bedrooms, photos, descriptions, prices, agent info), host-site cookies, browsing history, or device fingerprints. See the Privacy Policy and Transparency page for the full data-practices rundown.
