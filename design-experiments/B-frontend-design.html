A field guide to the badge

Read every
NZ listing
through risk

You’ve installed the WhareScore Badge. From now on, listings on homes.co.nz, OneRoof and realestate.co.nz are annotated with the same analysis that powers wharescore.co.nz. Nothing on the page is copied or stored — only the address is sent.

01

How to begin, in three small motions

  1. i. Pin

    Hang it on the toolbar

    Open Chrome’s puzzle-piece menu and pin WhareScore Badge. The popup is where per-site toggles and the 24-hour pause live. Pin it; never hunt for it again.

  2. ii. Visit

    Open any supported listing

    Pull up an address on homes.co.nz, OneRoof, or realestate.co.nz. A small badge slides in at the bottom-right of the page carrying that address’s WhareScore — the headline number, in context.

  3. iii. Read on

    Then dig deeper

    Click View full report for the complete risk analysis. Sign in to save the property to your watchlist; upgrade to Pro for price & rent estimates, walk score and PDF export.

02

Where it appears

  • homes.co.nz/address/*
  • OneRoof/property/*
  • realestate.co.nz/residential/sale/*
  • Trade MeTemporarily disabled — Angular mount under review
03

What you control

  • Click the toolbar icon to pause the badge for 24 hours.
  • Toggle any site off in the popup — the badge stops appearing there.
  • Click × on the badge to dismiss it for a specific address (we remember that decision for 7 days).
  • Drag the badge by its header to move it; the position is remembered per site.
  • Uninstall through Chrome’s standard extension manager. All extension state is discarded on uninstall.
04

What we do not collect

No listing content. No host-site cookies. No browsing history. No device fingerprints.

We don’t copy bedrooms, photos, descriptions, prices, or agent details. The address is the entire payload. See the Privacy Policy and the Transparency page for the full data-practices rundown.
